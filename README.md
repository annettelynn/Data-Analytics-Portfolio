## 2. My current projects

### [Project 1: Ads Campaign Performance - Top 15 The Analyst Challenge](https://app.powerbi.com/view?r=eyJrIjoiZWRjZDllNDktMzI2OC00NWVhLTkxMDctNGEzNjIxMDU2M2QyIiwidCI6IjkxMTg1MWRjLTc5MTItNGY4OC1hMGU3LWI3YTRiYjNmYjlmYSIsImMiOjEwfQ%3D%3D)
An analysis of an ad campaign efficacy for an agency overseeing 20 client companies from 2022 to 2026, as part of my participation in The Analyst Challenge. Utilising SQL for cohort analysis—focusing on spend quartiles and campaign ID returns—and Power BI for visualisation, I derived measures such as profit, CPC, CPA, and CTR. My findings revealed that, despite a 316% ROI, higher spend did not enhance profit or conversions; lower spend correlated with a 32.39% CTR. Notably, the agency’s client base declined from 2022 to 2026, with most campaigns launched in 2022, suggesting productivity challenges. Additionally, I identified Pinterest’s high spend yet low profit in the fashion segment, contrasted by higher profits from shorter campaign durations.
